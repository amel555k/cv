Tvoja Maja

React Node.js
View on GitHub

Tvoja Maja is a web-based clothing store built around a simple and intuitive shopping experience. Customers can browse available t-shirts, hoodies, and polo shirts, customize their order through a dedicated editor, and submit it with ease. Each registered user has access to their own profile where they can review past orders, while a contact form allows them to leave feedback or suggestions directly to the team.

On the administrative side, the app includes a fully featured admin panel that provides an overview of all placed orders along with key order statistics. Admins can monitor and update the availability status of individual items and read messages submitted by users — giving full control over both inventory and customer communication.

About Me

I'm a second-year Software Engineering student from Travnik, Bosnia, currently studying at the university in Zenica. Coming from a small city, I've always been driven by curiosity and a passion for building things that actually work and look great.

I have a particular interest in mobile app design and development, though at the core of everything I do lies a strong preference for backend logic and well-structured functionality. I enjoy thinking through how systems work under the hood just as much as crafting a clean user interface.

When I'm not writing code, you'll find me outdoors — whether that's cycling through the countryside, hitting the gym, or simply enjoying what nature around Travnik has to offer.
